Livelihood Retail shop Scheme 01

Term Loan · Purpose: business

Scheme Name: Livelihood Retail shop Scheme 01

Livelihood Retail shop Scheme 01 provides concessional finance for retail shop under Ministry of Social Justice Linked Livelihood Desk. Beneficiaries from category SC meeting income and age criteria may apply through the listed channel partner. This scheme is open to both male and female beneficiaries.

Availability: Lifetime

Eligible activity: Retail shop

Beneficiary category: SC

Target gender: any

Age limit: 18 to 55 years

Annual family income: Rs. 1.50 lakh

Minimum loan: Rs. 25,000

Maximum loan: Rs. 2 lakh

Interest rate: 4.0%

Repayment period: 24 months

Moratorium: 3 months

Required documents: Aadhaar, Identity proof, Address proof, Income certificate, Caste certificate, Bank account, Project report, Passport size photograph

Application process: Submit the application form with required documents at the authorised channel partner office. After verification, the sanction letter is issued as per applicable guidelines.
