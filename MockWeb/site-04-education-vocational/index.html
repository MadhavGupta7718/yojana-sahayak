Shiksha Educational Loan Scheme 01

Educational Loan · Purpose: education

Scheme Name: Shiksha Educational Loan Scheme 01

Shiksha Educational Loan Scheme 01 provides concessional finance for professional degree under Skill and Higher Education Credit Cell. Beneficiaries from category SC meeting income and age criteria may apply through the listed channel partner. This scheme is open to both male and female beneficiaries.

Availability: Lifetime

Eligible activity: Professional degree

Beneficiary category: SC

Target gender: any

Age limit: 18 to 35 years

Annual family income: Rs. 1.50 lakh

Minimum loan: Rs. 50,000

Maximum loan: Rs. 3 lakh

Interest rate: 4.0%

Repayment period: 24 months

Moratorium: 3 months

Required documents: Aadhaar, Identity proof, Address proof, Income certificate, Caste certificate, Bank account, Educational certificate, Passport size photograph

Application process: Submit the application form with required documents at the authorised channel partner office. After verification, the sanction letter is issued as per applicable guidelines.
